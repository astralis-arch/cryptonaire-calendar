The lead

JPMorgan sees bitcoin capitulation as Strategy discount hits record

Shares in Strategy, the largest corporate bitcoin holder, briefly traded at a record discount to its bitcoin, which JPMorgan called a sign of capitulation, The Block reported.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 26 Nov 2026

JPMorgan said on Wednesday that Strategy's premium to the value of its bitcoin had fallen to about 0.88 times, a record low and below the late-June trough, which it described as a sign of bitcoin capitulation, The Block reported.

Strategy's own dashboard showed the ratio at 0.88 on Wednesday morning in New York before it recovered to 0.94. JPMorgan also noted that BlackRock's IBIT fund kept losing money even during the brief price recovery, and put bitcoin's estimated production cost at about $86,000.

A separate on-chain reading pointed the same way. The share of bitcoin that has not moved for at least a year rose to 68.5%, from 62% in July, according to Glassnode data reported by CoinDesk. Similar rises came before past bear-market bottoms, though they gave little guide to timing.

Not every signal agreed. Bitcoin's 50-day average crossed below its 200-day average, a so-called death cross, Decrypt reported. Economist Robin Brooks warned of a possible 'meltdown' in risk assets before the Fed meets on 8 and 9 December, Cointelegraph reported, with FedWatch showing almost no chance of rates staying on hold.

Bitcoin traded near $60,870 on Kraken at 21:30 UK, up from about $60,380 at the afternoon edition, with US markets shut for Thanksgiving. Zcash recovered to near $721 from $698. About $7 billion of bitcoin options expire on Deribit at 08:00 UK on Friday.

One thing explained

What a 'death cross' is

A death cross is when a short-term moving average, usually the 50-day, falls below a long-term one, usually the 200-day. It shows that recent prices have been lower than the longer trend.

Because both lines are averages of past prices, the cross arrives well after a fall has started. Bitcoin is already about half its October 2025 high, so the signal mostly confirms what has happened rather than forecasting what comes next.
