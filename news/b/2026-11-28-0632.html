The lead

Bitcoin closes the day above warning level in quiet night

Bitcoin ended Friday just under $60,000 and barely moved overnight, as a research firm played down fears that AI could break its cryptography.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 28 Nov 2026

Bitcoin closed Friday at about $59,864, according to Kraken data, roughly $1,340 above the $58,525 daily-close level Galaxy had flagged as a warning sign. It then traded between about $59,540 and $60,200 through the Asian session and stood near $59,790 at 06:30 UK on Saturday.

The quiet night followed Friday's sharp drop at the US open, when bitcoin briefly fell to about $58,700 and leveraged positions were liquidated.

Research firm K33 said rapid progress by AI in mathematics warranted attention to bitcoin's cryptography, but that there was no evidence the network's security had been broken, Decrypt reported. The note followed a draft proof of the Riemann Hypothesis published last week with help from an AI model; K33 said it offered no method to attack bitcoin's signatures.

Zcash traded near $741, little changed overnight and holding Friday's gain of about 8%, Kraken data showed.

In fund news, Franklin Templeton will add Hyperliquid's HYPE token to its Franklin Crypto Index ETF on or about 1 December, according to an SEC filing reported by Cointelegraph. No UK or US economic data is due over the weekend; the weekly close comes at midnight on Sunday.
