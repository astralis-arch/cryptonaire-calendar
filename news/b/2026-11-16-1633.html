The lead

Wall Street's tech slide deepens as bitcoin holds its ground

The Nasdaq fell up to 2% as chip stocks extended their losses before Nvidia's results, but bitcoin held near $64,600 while crypto shares sank.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 16 Nov 2026

US technology shares fell sharply on Monday as the sell-off in Asian chipmakers spread to Wall Street. The Nasdaq Composite was down 1.98% at 15:09 UTC, according to Yahoo Finance data cited by cryptoticker, after Reuters reported a fall of more than 1% at the open.

Nvidia shed about 2% ahead of its third-quarter results on Tuesday, its first since Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive. The Financial Times reported that Huang and allies were discussing a campaign to reinstate him, and President Trump said bringing him back "certainly isn't a bad idea", Yahoo Finance reported.

Bitcoin held up better than shares. It traded near $64,650 on Kraken at 16:30 UK, about where it stood in the morning, after dipping to about $63,840 shortly before midday UK and briefly touching $65,630 around the US open. Crypto stocks fell harder, with Coinbase down about 6% and Strategy about 7%.

Options traders are leaning defensive. The $60,000 put is now the largest bitcoin options position on Deribit, at about $900 million in notional value, overtaking the $100,000 call, CoinDesk reported. The Crypto Fear and Greed Index stood at 10, extreme fear.

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh attends a San Francisco Fed hearing on AI and productivity this evening, UK time, and US spot bitcoin ETF flows for Monday follow after the close.

One thing explained

Why crypto shares fall harder than bitcoin

Companies such as Coinbase and Strategy are listed shares, so they are sold along with the rest of the stock market on days like this. Their earnings or balance sheets also depend on crypto prices and trading volumes, which adds a layer of leverage on top of bitcoin's own move.

So a 1% move in bitcoin can come with a 6% or 7% move in these shares, as happened today. A large gap like this shows the selling is coming from stock-market investors rather than from bitcoin holders.

Zcash dips to about $846 as the grants vote opens

Zcash touched about $846 on Kraken and stood near $883 at 16:30 UK. The non-binding vote, open until 30 November 23:59 UTC, centres on moving 50,000 ZEC from the Zcash Community Grants treasury to the Network Sustainability Mechanism, which pays it back to miners over years, cryptoticker reported. Any change would need the NU7.1 upgrade.

CoinGecko showed a midday low near $860; cryptoticker says the vote coincided with the drop but is not confirmed as its cause.
