Bitcoin slides towards summer low as Nvidia shock hits tech

Bitcoin fell below $58,000 in late US trading, within about 1% of its June-July low, as Nvidia shares sank on a report of an SEC accounting probe.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 20 Nov 2026

Bitcoin fell as low as about $57,660 on Friday evening, Kraken data showed, extending the break below $60,000 that came after the US open. It traded near $58,600 at 21:30 UK time, about 5% down on the day.

CoinDesk reported that the drop erased the August-September rebound and left Bitcoin within about 2% of its June-July low near $57,700. About $1.5 billion of leveraged crypto positions were liquidated over 24 hours, according to CoinGlass data cited by CoinDesk, roughly double the figure at midday.

The selling came as Nvidia fell about 13% to a 52-week low of $126.64, Decrypt reported, after Reuters said SEC enforcement staff were examining possible accounting issues. The SEC declined to comment.

Sentiment sank further. The Crypto Fear and Greed Index fell to 9, its lowest since February, Decrypt said. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC he would encourage the Fed not to back a half-point rise in December, Bitcoin Magazine reported; CME FedWatch showed a more than 98% chance of a quarter-point move.

Zcash fell harder still, to about $737 on Kraken, down about 17% since midnight. Traders now await Friday's ETF flow figures on Saturday morning, after November's outflows reached about $3.47 billion.

One thing explained

Why liquidations snowball

Traders who borrow to bet on rising prices are forced out by the exchange when the price falls far enough. Their positions are sold automatically, which pushes the price lower and can trigger the next batch of forced sales.

That is why falls through round numbers like $60,000 can speed up suddenly. Once most leveraged bets are cleared, the forced selling stops, which is why analysts watch liquidation totals for signs of exhaustion.
