The lead

Bitcoin climbs back above $61,000 before monthly options expiry

Bitcoin rose about $800 overnight in thin holiday trade, while Zcash gained 5% as a rival privacy coin lost another exchange listing.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 27 Nov 2026

Bitcoin traded near $61,680 on Kraken at 06:30 UK on Friday, up about $800 from the previous evening, after a jump at around 02:00 UK took it to an overnight high of $61,925, according to Kraken data.

The move came with US markets shut for Thanksgiving and Asian volumes light, and FXStreet described it as a tentative rebound. About $7 billion of bitcoin options expire on Deribit at 08:00 UK, which traders have watched for a pull towards $60,000, where puts cluster.

Zcash rose about 5% to near $758, recovering most of Thursday's fall. KuCoin said it will delist Monero on 30 November and close withdrawals on 31 December, Cointelegraph reported, citing the EU's ban on anonymity-enhancing coins at regulated firms from July 2027. Grayscale's head of research, Zach Pandl, said the firm's commitment to Zcash would continue 'for many years to come'.

Separately, Binance said its withdrawal suspension from the 29 October hack had been fully lifted, CoinDesk reported. The exchange said in early November it had restored 98% of the $6.34 billion affected.

In the UK, Coinbase's European policy head Katie Harries said the FCA was 'getting its approach to DeFi wrong', Cointelegraph reported. FCA final guidance is expected in January. US stock markets reopen for a half-day session closing at 18:00 UK.

One thing explained

Why Monero is being delisted but Zcash mostly is not

Monero hides every transaction by default, so an exchange cannot see where coins came from. Zcash lets users choose: transparent addresses work much like bitcoin, and only shielded ones are private.

Exchanges under anti-money-laundering rules, such as the EU's ban from July 2027, find default privacy hardest to accept. Optional privacy has so far kept Zcash on more venues, though the EU rule covers anonymity-enhancing coins broadly.

What we are investigating

  • Will the EU ban from July 2027 catch ZEC at EU exchanges as well as Monero? Evidence: exchange notices naming ZEC, or EU guidance on what counts as an anonymity-enhancing coin.
  • Does the bounce hold after expiry? Evidence: bitcoin's price around 08:00 UK and the first US ETF flow figures after Thanksgiving, due this evening.
  • Will the FCA respond to Coinbase's criticism of its DeFi plans? Evidence: an FCA statement; none in its feed overnight.
