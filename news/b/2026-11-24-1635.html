The lead

Record ETF exodus keeps bitcoin pinned below $60,000

Investors pulled about $570 million from US spot bitcoin funds on Monday, taking November's outflows to a record, and the price failed to follow stocks higher on Tuesday.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 24 Nov 2026

US spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded about $570 million of net outflows on Monday, The Block reported, confirming early aggregator estimates. CoinDesk put November's total withdrawals at a record $4.71 billion, while The Block said the month's tally was nearing $5 billion.

Bitcoin traded near $59,470 on Kraken at 16:33 UK, about 1.5% below the morning edition and under $60,000 for most of the day, after a 24-hour low of about $59,310. CoinDesk reported that liquidations tripled as the coin handed back all of Monday's Nvidia-driven gains.

The weakness came even as wider markets rose. The Conference Board said its US consumer confidence index jumped to 103.3 in November from 91.9, its highest since January 2025 and well above the 92.5 economists expected, according to Finwire. CoinDesk reported that the stock rally failed to budge crypto.

Zcash fared worse. ZEC fell to about $712 on Kraken, roughly 7% below the morning edition, a day after Grayscale's new leveraged Zcash fund dropped more than 13% on its debut.

Attention now turns to Wednesday, when US PCE inflation and a revised estimate of third-quarter growth are due at 13:30 UK, before US markets close on Thursday for Thanksgiving.

One thing explained

Why ETF outflows can weigh on price

A spot bitcoin ETF holds real bitcoin for its shareholders. When more money leaves a fund than arrives, the fund usually has to sell bitcoin to pay those investors back, so a long run of net outflows adds steady selling to the market.

That is why daily flow figures are watched so closely: they show whether big, slower-moving investors are adding or cutting exposure, which can matter more than any single day's headline.
