The lead

Nvidia beats forecasts as bitcoin claws back to $64,500

The chipmaker's first report since its chief executive resigned topped estimates, lifting its shares after hours as bitcoin recovered from a day low near $63,300.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 17 Nov 2026

Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion on Tuesday, up 103% from a year earlier, according to the company's results release, ahead of the roughly $109 billion analysts had expected. It forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, in a quarter that includes an extra week.

Its shares rose about 2.3% in after-hours trading to around $159, 24/7 Wall St. reported. It was the first report under interim chief executive Colette Kress, who took over after Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November. Kress said the new Vera Rubin chips were off to an explosive start.

Wall Street had fallen again before the results. Near the close the Dow was about 1.7% lower, the S&P 500 down about 1.2% and the Nasdaq down about 1%, Bitcoin Magazine reported. Strategy, the largest corporate holder of bitcoin, fell more than 5% to about $97, its first close below $100 since mid-August.

Bitcoin traded near $64,550 on Kraken at 21:30 UK, up from about $63,800 at the afternoon edition, after touching about $63,270 during the day.

Glassnode said in its weekly report that losses being realised by holders relative to profits had reached levels last seen near the 2018, 2020, 2022 and early 2023 lows, Decrypt reported. The firm said a daily close below $62,000 could open a drop towards $58,500, while a move above $74,100 would point to a bottom forming.

Attention now turns to UK inflation figures at 07:00 UK on Wednesday and the minutes of the Fed's October meeting at 19:00 UK.

One thing explained

Realised losses

When a coin moves on chain, analysts compare its price now with its price when it last moved. If it is lower, the seller has realised a loss. Adding these up across the network shows how much holders are selling below what they paid.

When realised losses are very large compared with realised profits, it usually means recent buyers are giving up. Firms such as Glassnode watch this because past cycle lows came after such bursts, but it shows pressure, not timing, and a burst can repeat before a low is in.

Trezor shipping partner breach exposes 27,000 customers' addresses

A breach at Skladon, a Trezor delivery partner, exposed names, phone numbers, emails, home addresses and products bought for orders placed from 9 August to 7 November, Bitcoin Magazine reported. Trezor said no wallet backups, PINs or passphrases were involved and that it never cold-calls customers. Coming a day after the Optimizely address-swap attack, it is a second reminder to treat unexpected contact about crypto orders as a scam.

The report did not say whether UK customers were included; Skladon ships to many countries but not the US.
