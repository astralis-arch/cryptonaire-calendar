Asia follows Wall Street lower as bitcoin slips before Nvidia

Regional stock markets fell around 1% overnight on chip weakness, and bitcoin gave back Monday evening's gain to trade near $64,400.

Asian shares fell on Tuesday as the sell-off in artificial intelligence stocks spread from Wall Street. Japan's Nikkei 225 lost 1.2%, Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell 1.2% and Australia's ASX 200 dropped 1.5%, the Associated Press reported. SoftBank fell 6.2%.

Nvidia reports third-quarter results after the US close on Tuesday, its first since Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive. Interim chief executive Colette Kress is due to speak, AP reported. US retail sales figures are due at 13:30 UK.

One thing explained

Why a strong dollar weighs on bitcoin

Bitcoin is priced in dollars. When the dollar strengthens, usually because US interest rates are expected to rise, holding cash or Treasuries pays more and dollar-priced assets look dearer to buyers elsewhere.

That is why a dollar index at a 19-month high and a priced-in Fed rise have gone hand in hand with bitcoin's slide this month. For a UK holder, a stronger dollar also means the sterling value of bitcoin falls less than the dollar price.

Zebra 7.1.0 patches ten security flaws; operators told to upgrade now

The Zcash Foundation's Zebra 7.1.0 fixes ten advisories, mostly ways a peer could stall a node's sync or exhaust its memory. Node operators, miners, pools, exchanges and wallet servers should upgrade now; no resync is needed and ZEC holders have nothing to do. Zcash traded near $915 on Kraken at 06:30 UK, up from about $900 at the evening edition.
