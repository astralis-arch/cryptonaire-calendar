The lead

Bitcoin fades after US open as Anthropic shares start trading

Bitcoin briefly topped $64,000 as tech futures rallied ahead of Anthropic's Nasdaq debut, then gave the gains back once US trading began.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 19 Nov 2026

Bitcoin traded at about $62,985 on Kraken at 16:30 UK on Thursday, about 1% below the morning edition, after reaching $64,014 shortly before the US open and then falling to $62,711 in the following hour.

The morning rise came as Nasdaq futures climbed about 2% ahead of Anthropic's first day as a listed company, according to Yahoo Finance. Bitcoin Magazine and CoinDesk both linked bitcoin's move towards $64,000 to the lift in tech shares.

Anthropic's founders rang the Nasdaq opening bell, The Next Web reported, and shares were eligible to trade from 15:00 UK. Before the open, perpetual futures on the shares traded near $104, about 25% above the $83 offer price, CryptoTicker reported.

CoinDesk said analysts disagree on whether investors are selling bitcoin to buy the new shares. It also reported that US spot bitcoin funds have seen outflows on 12 of the past 14 days, and that the largest cluster of leveraged long positions that could be forced to close sits near $62,030.

In a speech on Thursday, Fed governor Philip Jefferson said a stronger reaction to inflation is warranted if expectations risk becoming unanchored, but gave no signal on December's decision.

One thing explained

What a liquidation cluster is

Traders who borrow to bet on a rise must add money or be closed out if the price falls far enough. Exchanges do this automatically, selling their position. When many such positions share a similar trigger price, analysts call it a liquidation cluster.

If the price reaches the cluster, forced selling can push it lower still for a short time. CoinDesk put the largest long cluster on Binance near $62,030 today, just below the level bitcoin tested last night.
