The lead

Bitcoin slips to four-month low as ETF selling deepens

The world's largest cryptocurrency fell to about $61,200 overnight as investors pulled $582 million from US bitcoin funds on Thursday.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 20 Nov 2026

Bitcoin fell to about $61,200 on Kraken in the early hours of Friday, its lowest level since early July, before steadying near $61,800 by 06:40 UK time.

The drop followed Thursday's sharp fall on Wall Street after Anthropic's record stock market debut. CoinDesk reported that the AI company's shares opened at $111.50 against an $83 offer price but closed at $97.17, as the Nasdaq fell 2.4%. Nasdaq futures were a further 0.7% lower in Asian hours, CoinDesk said.

Crypto futures worth more than $600 million were liquidated over 24 hours, nearly half of them in the last 12, according to CoinDesk.

US spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded net outflows of $582 million on Thursday, nearly twice Wednesday's figure, according to SoSoValue data reported by Bloomingbit. BlackRock's IBIT accounted for $541 million. It was the third straight day of withdrawals, taking the three-day total to $1.13 billion.

Pressure also showed in Bitcoin-linked shares. Strategy closed down 4.6% at $86.13, its sixth straight fall, Bitcoin Magazine reported, leaving its 850,849 coins about $11 billion below their cost.

Separately, VanEck told Gemini it would end custody of its bitcoin, ether and solana ETFs from about 16 February 2027, CoinDesk reported. Traders now look to the US open at 14:30 UK.

One thing explained

Why a big stock listing can hit crypto

A record share sale like Anthropic's needs tens of billions of dollars of buyers. Funds that want a slice often sell other holdings, usually liquid, volatile assets such as tech stocks and Bitcoin funds, to make room. When the new shares then fall from their opening price, the whole risk basket can sell off together.

That spillover is usually short-lived, which is why traders watch whether Bitcoin keeps falling once tech stocks stabilise.
