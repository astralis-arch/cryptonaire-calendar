Bitcoin climbs back above $61,000 on a quiet Sunday

Bitcoin reversed its overnight dip during UK hours, while Kraken said a weekend price collapse in its Canadian-dollar market was a market glitch, not a breach.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 22 Nov 2026

Bitcoin traded near $61,190 on Kraken at 16:30 UK time on Sunday, Kraken data showed, about 3.9% above its level at the morning edition. Most of the gain came between 08:00 and 10:00 UK, and it touched a high near $61,590 around noon.

No single piece of news was found to explain the rise, which came with US stock and ETF markets closed for the weekend. Bitcoin remains well above Friday's two-year low of $55,130.

Cointelegraph reported that Kraken's Canadian-dollar markets saw heavy selling from Friday evening, briefly putting Bitcoin at about C$24,250, or roughly $16,900, on Saturday morning, while its US-dollar market was near $57,650. Kraken said every order was processed correctly, it found no breach, and executed trades stand.

Separately, Upbit and Bithumb halted CYBER deposits and withdrawals after Cyber reported unexpected token minting and funds leaving its bridge, Cointelegraph reported. It follows Saturday's roughly $12 million Chainflip hack.

Zcash rose to near $794 from about $784 this morning, according to Kraken. US stock futures reopen at about 23:00 UK, with US markets and spot bitcoin ETFs trading again from 14:30 UK on Monday.

One thing explained

Why one exchange pair can crash on its own

Bitcoin trades against many currencies. A pair like BTC/CAD usually tracks BTC/USD because market makers and arbitrage traders keep the two in line using the US-dollar to Canadian-dollar rate. If a key market maker stops quoting or runs out of funds, a large sell order can hit very few buyers and print a far lower price on that pair alone.

That is why one venue's chart can show a wild wick that other markets never saw. It reflects missing liquidity in that pair, not a change in what Bitcoin is worth elsewhere.
