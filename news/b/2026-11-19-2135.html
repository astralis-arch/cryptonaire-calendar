Bitcoin breaks $62,000 as Anthropic debut turns into tech selloff

A blockbuster listing that lifted futures in the morning ended with Wall Street sharply lower and bitcoin at its weakest level since July.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 19 Nov 2026

Bitcoin fell below $62,000 on Thursday and traded at about $61,510 on Kraken at 21:30 UK, after touching $61,426. That was about 2% below the afternoon edition and about 2.3% down on the day.

Bitcoin Magazine said the low was the weakest since July and put the fall from 19 October at about 25%. It linked the drop to a broad tech selloff that followed Anthropic's Nasdaq debut, with the Dow down about 1,000 points late in the session.

Anthropic's shares opened at $111.50, 34% above the $83 offer price, CoinDesk reported, briefly valuing the company above $2 trillion. They then traded as low as $95.05, Cointelegraph reported, and were near $97 in late trading. CoinDesk said the Nasdaq, S&P 500 and Dow were each down more than 1.5% in the afternoon.

Crypto-linked shares fell further. Bitcoin Magazine reported Strategy down about 4% and Galaxy Digital down about 6.9%. Decrypt said US spot bitcoin funds had lost about $2.9 billion in November, including $306.4 million on Wednesday.

The fall took bitcoin through the $62,030 area where CoinDesk had earlier placed the largest cluster of leveraged long positions. Thursday's ETF flow figures are due on Friday morning.

One thing explained

What a death cross is

A death cross is when a short-term moving average of price, usually the 50-day, falls below a long-term one, usually the 200-day. It shows that recent prices have been weaker than the longer trend.

Because both averages are built from past prices, the signal arrives after much of the fall has already happened. Decrypt reported one on bitcoin today using exponential averages, which give more weight to recent days.

Sky's USDS stablecoin loses over $1.5 billion of reserves, peg holds

More than $1.5 billion of USDC left Sky's reserve module after Inca Digital flagged $518 million of illicit-linked transactions, over $100 million attributed to North Korean actors, CoinDesk reported. USDS supply fell by more than 30% to $3.2 billion while holding $1. Sky said flagged activity was about 0.03% of total volume.

CoinDesk's headline says 35%; its report says more than 30%.

Our read · swingA large stablecoin shrinking fast in a falling market is a stress signal for DeFi liquidity, though the peg holding shows redemptions are being met.

Another explanationMuch of the outflow came from one large holder, so it may reflect a single exit rather than broad loss of trust.

What would change thisA break in the $1 peg, or the remaining roughly $1 billion of reserves draining, would make this a wider market story.
