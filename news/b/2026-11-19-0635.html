Bitcoin holds $62,000 test as Anthropic prices record listing

Bitcoin dipped to its weekend low late on Wednesday and recovered overnight, as Anthropic priced the largest share sale on record ahead of its Nasdaq debut today.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 19 Nov 2026

Bitcoin traded at about $63,570 on Kraken at 06:30 UK on Thursday, up about 2% from the evening edition, after falling to exactly $62,000 shortly after 22:00 UK on Wednesday and then recovering through the Asian session.

Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 a share, above its indicated range of $70 to $78, raising $81.3 billion and valuing the company at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis, Reuters reported, citing a source. The shares are due to begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday.

The listing matters for bitcoin because the two have been trading closely: bitcoin's 30-day correlation with Nvidia shares had risen to 0.56, Block Scholes said, as reported earlier this week.

Selling through US spot bitcoin funds continued. They recorded net outflows of $306.4 million on Wednesday, the second straight day, with $148.7 million leaving BlackRock's IBIT, TokenPost reported, citing SoSoValue data.

Separately, court documents showed that bitcoin worth about $1.42 billion was among the $6.3 billion stolen from Binance in October, Cointelegraph reported. A hearing on 1 December will consider extending the court's freezing order.

One thing explained

What it means when an IPO prices above its range

Before a listing, a company and its banks set an indicative price range and take orders from big investors. If orders far exceed the shares on offer, the final price can be set above that range, as Anthropic's was.

A price above range signals strong demand, but it also raises the bar for the first day of trading: buyers have already paid more, so less of the 'pop' is left on the table.
