The lead

Bitcoin steadies near $65,000 ahead of the weekly close

A quiet Sunday evening left bitcoin little changed in a $1,000 range, while Zcash's community prepared for Monday's treasury vote.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 15 Nov 2026

Bitcoin traded between about $64,230 and $65,170 on Kraken from 16:00 to 21:30 UK on Sunday and stood near $64,940 at 21:30, little changed from $64,640 at the afternoon edition.

The price remains well below the $66,090 reached on Sunday morning and above the overnight low of about $61,970. The weekly candle closes at midnight UK, and there was no new US spot ETF data over the weekend after Friday's seventh straight day of outflows.

Zcash held near $899, with an evening range of roughly $890 to $914 on Kraken. On the Zcash community forum, a member posted a draft proposal that would apply one test to both of Zcash's development treasuries, sending any money above each programme's commitments and a two-year reserve to the network's sustainability fund. The advisory poll on the future of Zcash Community Grants opens on Monday.

Elsewhere, The Block reported that a trader group claimed more than $6 million in profit by spotting a public code change at custody firm BitGo and buying two memecoins before Robinhood listed them. The Block said it could not verify the profit figures.

Attention now turns to the weekly close and Monday's ETF flows.

Draft Zcash proposal would cap both development treasuries

A forum post on Sunday proposed a draft ZIP applying one test to both the Zcash Community Grants and the coinholder-controlled fund, sending anything above each programme's commitments and a two-year reserve to the network sustainability fund, with caps and US dollar valuation. It is an early draft for comment; the advisory poll on Zcash Community Grants' future opens on Monday 16 November.

A single community member's draft, not an adopted proposal. The author said they will vote for the status quo in the advisory poll.

Our read · longerThe debate over how much new Zcash goes to development funds is widening, which affects long-run supply and who steers the project.

Another explanationDraft proposals are common on the forum and many never reach a vote, so this may change little.

What would change thisStrong support for a cap option in the advisory poll, or the draft being taken up by the Zcash Foundation or ECC.
