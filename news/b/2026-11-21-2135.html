Bitcoin holds above $60,000 in a quiet Saturday evening

Bitcoin kept most of Saturday's rebound into the evening, trading in a narrow band around $60,000 as traders waited for Monday's reopening of US markets.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 21 Nov 2026

Bitcoin traded near $60,260 on Kraken at 21:30 UK time on Saturday, Kraken data showed, about 0.5% above the level at the afternoon edition. Since 16:30 UK it has stayed between about $59,660 and $60,450.

CoinDesk reported that Bitcoin had risen more than 9% from an early low of $55,130, its weakest since September 2024. It cited Glassnode as saying Friday's sell-off had cleared the largest cluster of futures liquidation levels, between $59,000 and $62,000.

CoinDesk also reported about $1.1 billion of net withdrawals from US spot bitcoin ETFs over the past week. QCP Capital told it that a lasting recovery needed stable technology shares, calmer rates and a return of institutional money.

Zcash was steady near $790 on Kraken, inside a range of about $778 to $798 since the afternoon.

US stock futures reopen on Sunday evening, and US stocks and ETFs on Monday, the first test of the weekend's moves.

A quiet evening: Bitcoin held near $60,260 and ZEC near $790, keeping Saturday's rebound. The test is the reopening of US markets on Sunday night and Monday.

Glassnode: sell-off cleared the biggest liquidation cluster

CoinDesk reported Glassnode as saying Friday's fall cleared the largest cluster of futures liquidation levels, between $59,000 and $62,000, and CryptoQuant's Darkfost calling it extreme capitulation. CoinDesk counted about $1.8 billion of liquidations in 24 hours, $1.3 billion of them long bets. Less leverage near the price means fewer forced trades to amplify the next move.

CoinDesk's $1.8 billion liquidation total is lower than the $2.67 billion Bitcoin.com News reported this afternoon; both cite CoinGlass, over different 24-hour windows.
