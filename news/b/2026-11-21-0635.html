Bitcoin breaks summer low, sinks to two-year bottom near $55,000

Bitcoin fell through its June-July low in early Asian trading on Saturday, touching $55,100 on Kraken, as about $2.4 billion of leveraged crypto bets were wiped out in a day.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 21 Nov 2026

Bitcoin fell as low as $55,100 on Saturday morning, Kraken data showed, after the slide that began on Friday cut through the June-July low near $57,700. It traded near $56,000 at 06:30 UK time, about 4% below the level at Friday's evening edition.

The steepest leg came in the 03:00 UK hour, when the price dropped from about $58,500 to $56,300. CoinDesk reported that Bitcoin was at its lowest since September 2024 and down about 28% this quarter.

About $2.40 billion of crypto futures positions were liquidated over 24 hours, with more than $1 billion in Bitcoin and $545 million in Ether, according to CoinGlass data cited by CoinDesk. The largest single liquidation was a $51.7 million long Bitcoin bet.

The fall followed a broad sell-off on Wall Street on Friday. The Nasdaq Composite fell about 5% and the S&P 500 about 3.5%, Bitcoin Magazine reported, with Nvidia down about 12.5%. CoinDesk said analysts pointed to rising inflation-adjusted US Treasury yields and a more hawkish Fed outlook.

Zcash fell harder, to a low of about $678 on Kraken, and other large coins including XRP and Solana dropped about 9% in 24 hours, CoinDesk said. Thursday's US spot bitcoin ETF outflow was $582 million, The Block reported; Friday's figure had not been confirmed at publication.

One thing explained

Why weekend breaks can overshoot

Crypto trades every day, but the big buyers that usually step in, such as ETF desks and US funds, mostly work when US markets are open. At the weekend, fewer orders sit on the books, so a large sale or a wave of forced liquidations moves the price further than it would on a weekday.

That is why traders watch whether a weekend break holds once Monday's US session opens: a level reclaimed then can mean the move was mostly thin trading, while a level that stays broken suggests real selling.

What we are investigating

  • How large were Friday's US spot bitcoin ETF outflows as Bitcoin broke $60,000? Evidence: SoSoValue or Farside daily totals; Farside could not be read this morning.
  • Has the leverage now been cleared? Evidence: Bitcoin futures open interest and funding rates on Saturday and Sunday compared with Friday.
  • Do Bitcoin treasury companies or miners face pressure at these prices? Evidence: filings or statements from Strategy and listed miners after JPMorgan's $86,000 production cost estimate.
