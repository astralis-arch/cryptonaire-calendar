The lead

Bitcoin retakes $60,000 as Wall Street claws back early losses

A late recovery in US shares lifted bitcoin nearly $3,000 from its morning low, though analysts warned that the month remains its worst in over four years.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 25 Nov 2026

Bitcoin climbed back above $60,000 on Wednesday afternoon in New York, turning positive for the day after rallying nearly $3,000 from its session low, CoinDesk reported. Kraken showed it near $60,260 at 21:30 UK, against a low of about $57,210.

The move tracked a recovery in US stocks. The Nasdaq, down about 4% at the open after hotter-than-expected core PCE inflation and new US tariff proposals, was down less than 1% late in the session, while the Dow turned positive, Yahoo Finance reported. The ten-year Treasury yield fell to 4.82% as investors bought bonds.

CoinDesk noted that investor Michael Burry said he had covered his technology shorts, which traders read as a sign of how stretched the selling had become.

Zcash rebounded more sharply, with ZEC near $761 on Kraken, up about 10% since the afternoon.

The wider picture stayed weak. Bitcoin was down about 24% in November, its worst month since June 2022, CoinDesk reported. Galaxy withdrew its call that the bear market had ended and said a daily close below $58,525 would point to lower lows.

US markets close on Thursday for Thanksgiving and reopen for a half day on Friday, when monthly bitcoin options expire.

One thing explained

Realised price

Bitcoin's realised price is the average price at which every coin last moved on the blockchain. It is a rough guide to what the average holder paid.

When the market price falls towards it, as 21Shares suggested around $53,000, the typical holder is close to breaking even, which some analysts treat as a level where selling pressure tends to ease. It is a rule of thumb, not a floor.
