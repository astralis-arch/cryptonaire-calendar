Bitcoin claws back to $60,000 after weekend flush

Bitcoin recovered nearly 10% from its overnight low on Saturday, briefly topping $60,000 again, after a wave of forced selling took it to its lowest level since September 2024.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 21 Nov 2026

Bitcoin rose to about $60,880 on Kraken in the 13:00 UK hour on Saturday, Kraken data showed, from a low of $55,100 in the early morning. It traded near $59,950 at 16:30 UK time, about 7% above the level at the morning edition.

Reuters reported Bitcoin up about 2.1% at $60,192 at 15:16 UK time. Bitcoin.com News said the overnight fall had come with about $2.67 billion of leveraged positions liquidated in 24 hours, about $1.3 billion of it in Bitcoin, according to CoinGlass.

The rebound left Bitcoin back above the June-July low near $57,700, which it broke overnight. Zcash rose further, from about $685 to a high near $806 on Kraken, before easing to about $785.

The bounce followed a heavy week on Wall Street. Decrypt reported that the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.3% on Friday and about 10% over the week, while Nvidia dropped 14% on the day and closed 47.5% below its October record. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost more than $1.25 billion from Tuesday to Friday, Bitcoin.com News reported.

US markets reopen on Monday, the first chance for stock and ETF investors to react to the weekend's moves.

One thing explained

Why a market can snap back after a liquidation flush

When prices fall fast, leveraged long bets are closed automatically, and each forced sale pushes the price lower and triggers the next. Once most of that leverage is gone, there are few forced sellers left, so even modest buying can lift the price quickly.

Traders who bet on further falls can then be squeezed in turn, adding to the rebound. That is why a sharp drop and a sharp bounce often come together, and why the real test is whether ordinary buyers stay once the forced trades are done.
