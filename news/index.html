The lead

Bitcoin slips back to $60,000 as US open triggers long liquidations

A morning bounce above $62,000 unwound within an hour of Wall Street's reopening, even as technology stocks rallied.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 27 Nov 2026

Bitcoin fell from about $61,400 to a low of $58,729 on Kraken in the hour after US markets opened at 14:30 UK on Friday, according to Kraken data, and traded near $60,000 at 16:30 UK.

About $190 million of positions were forced shut in the hour of the drop, $182 million of them bets on rising prices, TokenPost reported. Ether fell about 4% to near $1,660 over the same hour.

The fall came as US stocks reopened higher after Thanksgiving. The Nasdaq 100 was up nearly 2% by late morning in New York, though Nvidia fell more than 2%, CoinDesk reported. No single crypto-specific trigger was reported.

The low stayed just above $58,525, the level at which Galaxy Digital said a daily close would mark 'the start of a move to a materially lower low'. In a report this week Galaxy withdrew its call that the bear market ended in June, Bitcoin Magazine reported. Glassnode said buy orders cluster between $50,000 and $58,000, according to CoinDesk.

Zcash fell from about $760 to $710 in the same move and recovered to near $739, still up on the day. US markets close early at 18:00 UK, and bitcoin is on course for its first weekly gain in eight weeks if it holds until Sunday's close, CoinDesk reported.

In brief

Bitcoin gave up its morning bounce at the US open, falling to $58,729 before steadying near $60,000 as about $190 million of longs were liquidated. The low stayed just above Galaxy's $58,525 warning level.

One thing explained

What a long liquidation is

Traders who borrow to bet on rising prices (longs) must keep enough collateral on the exchange. When the price falls far enough, the exchange closes the position automatically by selling.

Those forced sales push the price lower, which can trigger the next batch. That is why a drop like today's can happen in minutes and then stop once the leverage has been cleared.
