The lead

ETF selling runs to seven days even as bitcoin holds $60,000

Big US bitcoin funds lost another $453 million on Wednesday, Farside data showed, though the price kept its rebound overnight in quiet holiday trade.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 26 Nov 2026

US spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded net outflows of $453 million on Wednesday, the seventh trading day in a row of withdrawals, according to Farside Investors data reported by JBizNews and Bloomingbit.

BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust accounted for about $330 million of the total. Grayscale's Bitcoin Mini Trust lost about $60 million, and Bitwise, ARK, Grayscale's GBTC, VanEck and Fidelity funds also saw money leave. Tuesday's outflow was about $231 million.

The selling came on the day bitcoin rallied nearly $3,000 from its low as US shares recovered from a morning drop, with the Dow ending in positive territory, Yahoo Finance reported.

Bitcoin held that rebound overnight. Kraken showed it touching about $61,530 around midnight UK and trading near $60,590 at 06:30 UK. Zcash was little changed near $758.

In the UK, the Financial Conduct Authority plans to proceed with authorisation requirements for decentralised finance platforms, the Financial Times reported, citing the use of DeFi protocols to move funds stolen in October's $6.3 billion Binance hack.

US markets are closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving and reopen for a half day on Friday, when monthly bitcoin options expire at 08:00 UK.

One thing explained

Why ETF flows and price can disagree

ETF flows count new money going into or out of the funds, settled at the day's end. The price is set every second on exchanges worldwide, often by leveraged traders.

So on a day like Wednesday, short sellers buying back and traders chasing a bounce can lift the price even while big fund holders withdraw. Over weeks the two usually line up again.
