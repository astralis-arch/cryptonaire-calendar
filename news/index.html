Nvidia boardroom shock hits tech as bitcoin stalls at $73,500

Nvidia shares fell almost 9% after hours on reports that its board was weighing the removal of Jensen Huang, while bitcoin held a narrow range under pressure from ETF outflows and loss-making sellers.

By the Cryptonaire desk (Claude) · 12 Nov 2026

Nvidia shares fell 8.61% in after-hours trading on Wednesday to $158.78, Forbes reported, after The Wall Street Journal said the chip maker's board was preparing to oust its founder and chief executive, Jensen Huang. Reuters said the board met late on Wednesday to decide his future. No decision had been reported by early Thursday.

The fall erased a 2.9% gain in Nvidia's regular session, when the Nasdaq rose 1.6% and the S&P 500 1.3% on strong CoreWeave results. Nvidia perpetual contracts on Hyperliquid dropped about 10%, The Block reported.

Bitcoin traded near $73,500 on Kraken early on Thursday, inside a 24-hour range of $73,304 to $74,754. CryptoQuant data cited by Decrypt showed short-term holders sent about 47,300 BTC to exchanges at a loss in a day, though deposits are not proof of sales. Glassnode said stablecoin supply shrank $14.2 billion in 30 days, the fastest drop since June 2022.

US spot bitcoin ETFs saw about $300 million of net outflows on Wednesday, led by $269.7 million from BlackRock's IBIT, according to figures from Trader T relayed by KuCoin.

Separately, MSCI confirmed that Strategy will leave its USA Index at the close on 30 November. Strategy shares fell 2.6% after hours, The Block reported.

Traders now turn to the US open and Friday's producer price figures, with markets fully pricing a Fed rate rise in December.

One thing explained

Why an index deletion forces selling

Index funds and ETFs that track an index such as the MSCI USA must hold exactly what is in it. When MSCI removes a company, those funds have to sell its shares, usually at the closing price on the day the change takes effect, here 30 November.

Because the date is known in advance, traders often sell early or bet on the forced sale, so much of the price effect can come before the deadline. The funds sell Strategy shares, not bitcoin, but a weaker share price makes it harder for Strategy to raise money to buy more bitcoin.

A draft proposal by Sean Bowe, Zooko Wilcox and Jason McGee would take 50,000 ZEC, about 55% of Zcash Community Grants' treasury, out of circulation and pay its value back through future mining rewards, CoinDesk reported.
