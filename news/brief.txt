Cryptonaire Post, morning edition, Saturday the twenty first of November. Bitcoin broke through its summer low overnight. It fell to about fifty five thousand one hundred dollars around four in the morning UK time and is now near fifty six thousand, its lowest in about two years. About two point four billion dollars of leveraged bets were wiped out in a day. The fall followed a five percent drop in the Nasdaq on Friday. Zcash is near six hundred and ninety nine dollars, after touching about six hundred and seventy eight. The next big event is the Fed decision on the ninth of December at seven in the evening.
