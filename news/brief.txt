Cryptonaire Post, afternoon edition, Friday the twentieth of November. Bitcoin has broken below sixty thousand dollars for the first time since early July, touching about fifty eight thousand seven hundred after the US open, and is now near fifty nine thousand two hundred. The Nasdaq opened at its lowest since April as Anthropic shares slid towards their offer price, and Wall Street's fear gauge is at its highest since March. Zcash fell about nine percent since this morning to around seven hundred and seventy five dollars. Watch the US close at nine tonight. The next big event is the Fed decision on the ninth of December at seven in the evening. Information only, not advice.
