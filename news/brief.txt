Cryptonaire Post, afternoon edition, Sunday the twenty second of November. Bitcoin has bounced back. It is near sixty one thousand two hundred dollars, up about four percent since this morning, with no clear news behind the move. Zcash is near seven hundred and ninety four dollars, a little higher. Kraken says Saturday's brief collapse in its Canadian dollar market, to about seventeen thousand dollars, was not a hack, and the trades will stand. Two Korean exchanges paused transfers of the CYBER token after a suspected bridge exploit. US stock futures reopen around eleven tonight, and US markets open at half past two on Monday afternoon. The next big event is the Fed decision on the ninth of December at seven in the evening. Information only, not advice.
