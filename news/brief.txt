Cryptonaire Post, morning edition, Friday the twentieth of November. Bitcoin slipped to about sixty one thousand two hundred dollars overnight, its lowest since early July, and is now near sixty one thousand eight hundred. US bitcoin funds lost five hundred and eighty two million dollars on Thursday, a third day of outflows, mostly from BlackRock. Nasdaq futures are lower again after Thursday's tech selloff. Zcash fell about three and a half percent to around eight hundred and fifty dollars. Watch the US open at half past two this afternoon.
