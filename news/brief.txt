Cryptonaire Post, evening edition, Saturday the twenty first of November. A quiet evening. Bitcoin has held its rebound and is near sixty thousand two hundred and sixty dollars, up about half a percent since the afternoon, in a narrow range. Zcash is steady near seven hundred and ninety dollars. Analysts at Glassnode say Friday's sell-off cleared the biggest cluster of leveraged positions. The test comes when US stock futures reopen around eleven on Sunday night, and when US markets open at half past two on Monday afternoon. The next big event is the Fed decision on the ninth of December at seven in the evening. Information only, not advice.
