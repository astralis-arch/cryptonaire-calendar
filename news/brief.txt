Cryptonaire Post, evening edition. Bitcoin broke below sixty two thousand dollars this evening and was about sixty one thousand five hundred at half past nine, its weakest level since July. Anthropic's shares opened a third above their offer price, then faded, and the Nasdaq, the S and P and the Dow all fell more than one and a half percent. Zcash slipped to about eight hundred and eighty five dollars. Sky's U S D S stablecoin lost more than a billion and a half dollars of reserves but held its peg. Thursday's E T F flows come out tomorrow morning. Information only, not advice.
