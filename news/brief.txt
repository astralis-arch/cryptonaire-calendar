Cryptonaire Post, afternoon edition. A quiet Saturday for bitcoin, which dipped to about fifty eight thousand nine hundred dollars and recovered to around fifty nine thousand four hundred, staying above Galaxy's warning level. The main story is World Liberty Financial, the Trump family-linked project. Its token halved to a record low after a reserve wallet sold about twenty million dollars of ether, and its dollar stablecoin briefly slipped to ninety four cents. Justin Sun has called for both to be delisted. Zcash eased to about seven hundred and twenty dollars. The weekly close is at midnight on Sunday. Information only, not advice.
