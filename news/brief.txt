Cryptonaire Post, morning edition, Monday the twenty third of November. Bitcoin is near sixty one thousand five hundred dollars, up about one percent overnight. Zcash is near seven hundred and eighty six dollars, a little lower. New figures show about one point eight billion dollars left US spot bitcoin funds last week, the biggest weekly outflow since June. Traders have dropped bets on a half point Fed rate rise in December, though a quarter point rise is still expected. US markets and bitcoin funds open at half past two this afternoon. The next big event is the Fed decision on the ninth of December at seven in the evening. Information only, not advice.
