Cryptonaire Post, evening edition, Sunday the twenty second of November. A quiet evening. Bitcoin is near sixty thousand eight hundred dollars, down a little since the afternoon but still above sixty thousand and up about one percent on the day. Zcash is near seven hundred and ninety one dollars, little changed. Chainflip says one hundred and twenty three bitcoin were stolen in Saturday's hack, about twelve million dollars, and its bounty offer runs until twenty to seven on Monday evening. US stock futures reopen around eleven tonight, and US markets open at half past two tomorrow afternoon. The next big event is the Fed decision on the ninth of December at seven in the evening. Information only, not advice.
