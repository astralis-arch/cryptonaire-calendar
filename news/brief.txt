Cryptonaire Post, afternoon edition. Bitcoin rose just above sixty four thousand dollars this morning as tech shares rallied ahead of Anthropic's Nasdaq debut, then slipped back after the US open. It was about sixty three thousand dollars at half past four, close to last night's low of sixty two thousand. Anthropic's shares became tradable from three o'clock UK time. Binance won a worldwide court order freezing up to six and a half billion dollars of hacked funds. Zcash eased to about nine hundred and twenty dollars. Information only, not advice.
