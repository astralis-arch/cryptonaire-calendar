Cryptonaire Post, afternoon edition, Saturday the twenty first of November. Bitcoin has bounced hard. After falling to about fifty five thousand one hundred dollars overnight, it climbed back above sixty thousand around two in the afternoon UK time and is now near fifty nine thousand nine hundred and fifty, about seven percent up since this morning. Zcash rose about twelve percent to near seven hundred and eighty five dollars. The test comes on Monday, when US markets reopen after Friday's five percent fall in the Nasdaq. The next big event is the Fed decision on the ninth of December at seven in the evening. Information only, not advice.
